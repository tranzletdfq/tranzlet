FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What is a remittance tag?+

It is the reference attached to a remittance so the payment, proof and verification status can be tracked together.

When is my wallet credited?+

After the submitted payment is reviewed and approved, the corresponding NGN amount is credited to your Tranzlet wallet.

Can I withdraw to my Nigerian bank account?+

Yes. Eligible users can save a bank account and request a payout from their available NGN wallet balance.

How do I get started?+

Create an account, complete your profile and use the dashboard to start your first remittance tag.
